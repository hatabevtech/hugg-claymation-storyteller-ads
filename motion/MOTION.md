# Motion prompts: TumGard × H. pylori (Google Flow)

Mode: **Frames to Video** for every clip (not Ingredients: it redraws the scene and loses the approved keyframes).
Every clip is 8 s; the **Keep** column is what the edit uses. Frames live in `frames/final/` (L-* in `cast/`).
Test first: run **C06** (wave) and **C15a** (heal) on both Omni 1.1 Flash and Veo 3.1, pick the model that keeps the villains rigid, the clay texture, and the lehyam soaking in rather than spreading. Then use that model for all clips.

Every prompt ends with the same style line, already included below. If a clip morphs, re-roll it once; if it still morphs, shorten the action (fewer verbs), not the style line.

## Beat 1 · Hook

**C01 · S01 → S02** · start S01, end S02
```
A clay man sits on the edge of his bed at night in cool grey-blue light, holding an empty foil blister strip, his other hand pressed below his ribs. The camera pushes in slowly and steadily from a medium shot to a tight close-up of his face. As it moves in, his discomfort grows: brows pull up and together, eyes squeeze slightly, mouth tightens under his moustache. He breathes in, shoulders lifting a little. Nothing else moves. Silent, no dialogue, no music. Stop-motion claymation: plasticine texture constant, slightly stepped stop-motion movement, faces and bodies keep their exact shape and skin tone, no morphing, no melting, no new objects, no text.
```
