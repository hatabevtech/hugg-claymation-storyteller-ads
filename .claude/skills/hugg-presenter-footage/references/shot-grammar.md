# Presenter shot grammar (from YOGABODY presenter ads)

Studied: five YOGABODY Meta ads (Fix Your Feet, Bunions, Happy Feet, Hip Opening, Happy Back). This is grammar only. Never copy their presenter, set, wardrobe or branding.

## Constants within one ad
- **One set and one outfit**, start to finish.
- **One seated position for the whole ad** (on a stool, cross-legged on the floor, or in a chair). Demos are the only exception.
- **Bright, soft, high-key daylight**, with a slightly shallow depth of field.
- **Eye contact with the lens** almost always. The only exceptions are looking down at a body part or prop being shown.

## Shot sizes, alternated every 1–3 s
| Size | Framing | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Wide | full body, seated, room visible | setup lines, "here's what you'll learn", demos, the anchor |
| Medium | waist up | explaining, lists, most lines |
| Close-up | chest up, leaning toward the lens | emphasis, the turn, "the good news is", the CTA |
| Extreme close-up | face fills the frame, slight low angle | the hook, a shock line, a single-word punch |

- **Never use the same size twice in a row** for P shots that cut directly into each other. Alternate sizes so the jump reads as energy.
- **Most cuts are punch-ins on the same take.** Only the size changes; the set, pose and light do not.

## Gesture follows the words
- **Counting** ("number one/two/three", "three things"): fingers up, palm to camera, and the count must match.
- **Explaining:** open palms, hands at chest height.
- **Body part named:** touch, hold or press it. For hugg that means a hand flat on the upper stomach, the chest, or the throat.
- **Contrast or "not this, that":** one hand out, then the other.
- **Small or precise idea:** a pinch gesture.
- **CTA:** both index fingers pointing down ("link neeche"), then a wave goodbye on the last line.
- **Product line:** holding the product at chest height, label to camera. In hugg ads this is the real pack, comped in the edit if needed.

## Expression follows the beat
- **Hook / pain:** concerned, brows pulled together.
- **Myth / "you've tried everything":** raised brows, a slight head tilt, mildly sceptical.
- **Turn / insight:** intense, leaning in.
- **Good news / product:** warm smile.
- **CTA:** open, friendly, smiling.

## Special shots
- **Hook frame:** the most striking frame in the ad. An extreme close-up leaning into the lens, a low angle, or a strong pose. Black-and-white is optional, but colour carries meaning in hugg ads, so check first.
- **Demo:** the presenter does the action full-body (e.g. eating, holding the stomach, taking the dose), in the set or as a cutout on plain grey.
- **Cutout:** the presenter on a plain grey background with room around them for graphics (circle masks, stickers). Make it a separate P shot only when the edit needs it.

## Pacing
- **Presenter ≈ 50–60% of runtime.** The rest is cutaways.
- **The presenter returns at least every ~6 s.**
- The ad opens on the presenter (the hook) and closes on the presenter (the CTA and wave).
