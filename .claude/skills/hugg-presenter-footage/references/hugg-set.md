# The hugg presenter set (DRAFT, awaiting the user's approval)

One fixed set for every hugg presenter ad, so the look becomes recognisable. Once a plate is approved, it's saved as `presenter/set/SET.png` and reused; after that, don't describe the set from scratch again.

## Set plate prompt (empty set, 9:16)
```
A bright, calm, modern Indian home studio, empty, no people. Warm off-white walls, light oak wooden floor. Behind the seat: a low wooden console with two small green plants, a brass bowl and a few neutral books, softly out of focus. One simple light-wood stool with a cushioned seat in the centre of the frame, facing the camera. Soft natural daylight from a large window on the left, gentle fill, high-key and warm but not yellow. Clean, uncluttered, premium but homely. Shot at seated eye level, 35mm, slightly shallow depth of field, background softly blurred. Vertical 9:16, with the stool and the area around it inside the central 4:5. Photorealistic. No text, no logos, no clocks with numbers, no posters, no screens.
```

## Rules
- **Colour:** warm neutrals plus green from the plants. No grey-blue (that's the "before" colour in hugg ads), and no strong amber (that's TumGard's).
- **Nothing readable anywhere:** no book titles, no wall art with text.
- **The presenter sits on the stool** in every ad. Their posture can vary per ad (upright, leaning in, one leg crossed), but it stays fixed within one ad.
