# TumGard × H. pylori: presenter ad, script v1 (DRAFT)

*hugg · Meta video ads · 4:5 · about 65 s · Hinglish VO · Status: draft for review in the claude.ai project*
*Format reference: YOGABODY "Fix Your Feet" presenter ad (structure and edit grammar only; we don't copy its look or lines).*
*Presenter: AI-generated host. Product: TumGard.*

## Rules this draft follows
- **Approved claim lines are reused word for word** from the claymation ad (`storyboard.md`). They're marked **[A]**.
- **New lines that need a claim/regulatory check** are marked **[CHECK]**.
- **The host is a host.** They aren't a doctor, aren't in a white coat, and never say "it worked for me." An AI person giving a first-person testimonial would be a fabricated testimonial.
- **"What you've tried" goes after habits, not professionals.** Unlike the reference, nobody (doctors, antacids, antibiotics) is "selling you something" or "doesn't work." The antibiotic stays the good guy: *Sahi kiya.*
- **TumGard never shares a frame with a bacterium.**
- **Colours mean one thing.** Red = pain/damage. Mustard-olive = H. pylori. White-blue = antibiotic. Black lehyam with an amber glow = TumGard. Warm golden = after. The caption highlight colour must not be any of these (proposal: white captions with a single **pink #E8A3A0** keyword, "healthy lining").

## Notes on the choices
- **"Aadha, aadha, saath"** stands in for the reference's "three S's." *Saath* means both "sixty" and "together," which leads straight into the founder email and nutritionist line. It's a new phrase, so it needs a check, and the team may prefer to drop the name and keep only the dosage line.
- **Where personalisation comes in.** The reference ends on "you'll know exactly what to do based on your condition." Our version is the founder email plus a real nutritionist call. If the free diet plan is actually personalised, "aapke pet ke hisaab se" would sharpen this. **[CHECK]** whether that's true before adding it.
- **The clay mechanism shots come back as cutaways** (S06–S09, S15, S17), so the presenter ad and the clay ad share one visual world and one set of approved frames.
